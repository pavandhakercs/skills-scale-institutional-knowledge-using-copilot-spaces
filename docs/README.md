# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This directory contains the OctoAcme project management knowledge base and serves as the entry point for understanding how the organization approaches delivery work. OctoAcme follows a structured lifecycle that begins with project initiation, moves through planning and execution, and ends with release and continuous improvement. The process is designed to align stakeholders quickly, define clear ownership, and keep the team focused on measurable value rather than ambiguous scope.

During initiation, the team validates the business need, aligns with stakeholders, and creates a lightweight one-pager that captures the problem, goal, success metrics, timeline, and key risks. Once the initiative is approved, planning turns the concept into an actionable backlog with prioritized work, acceptance criteria, milestones, and dependencies. This ensures work is broken into shippable increments and managed with clear accountability from the start.

Execution is coordinated through a cross-functional rhythm of standups, weekly delivery updates, backlog tracking, and regular reviews. OctoAcme emphasizes defined roles: project managers coordinate schedules and communication, product leads define outcomes and priorities, developers build and test solutions, QA validates quality, and stakeholders provide input and approvals. Communication is managed through a single source of truth, regular status updates, and escalation paths when blockers or risks need leadership attention.

Quality and continuous improvement are treated as core practices rather than final-stage add-ons. The process includes Definition of Done, CI validation, testing gates, and release checks before deployment. After launch, teams conduct retrospectives to capture lessons learned, update risk and communication plans, and refine how work is managed moving forward. The documentation in this folder provides the guidance needed to operationalize that model consistently across projects.
